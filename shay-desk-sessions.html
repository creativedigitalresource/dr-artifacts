Day-of playbook

1
60 seconds off-camera before Q1 rolls

Chat about anything not on the list first. Nobody's first take of the day should be their real first answer — warm up his voice and energy before it's being recorded.

2
One continuous take per question

No interrupting for a redo. Keeps the low-lift setup and the natural energy that makes desk UGC work.

3
Bridge out loud instead of jumping to the next question cold

A one-line connector — "since you mentioned failure, what happens if a business stops marketing entirely?" — keeps it feeling like a conversation, not an interrogation. The order above is built so these bridges are always true, not forced.

4
Let the restart happen naturally, don't force it

If Shay's answer needs the question's context to stand alone, ask him to restate it — but don't make him echo it every time. A stiff, rehearsed-sounding restart hurts authenticity more than a caption in editing costs you.

5
3-second silent hold before and after each answer

Clean in/out points for the editor — no scrambling to find a cut.

6
Log the deliverable + submission date same day

Back in the tracker sheet, right after edit — keeps next session's planning trustworthy. For the Partner Program answer specifically, also flag Basecamp #8156787211 done once it's shot.
