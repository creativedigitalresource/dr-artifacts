Marketing-contact status is a HubSpot billing tier, not a consent flag — a contact can sit non-marketable without ever having opted out. 98.4% of this pool came in through a Salesforce sync (sales-sourced prospects, not a purchased list), so CAN-SPAM allows us to email them without prior opt-in as long as we identify the message as an ad and honor opt-outs. Rollout status: verified in HubSpot, the Cooling Drip has a real history here.
